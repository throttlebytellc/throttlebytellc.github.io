Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Tiny Tent is developed and operated by Caleb Davis. For privacy questions or requests, contact mossmallowsupport@gmail.com.

Game progress stays on your device

The app stores puzzle marks, undo history, active play time, hint use, completed levels, best star ratings, daily activity, settings, and ad-related hint entitlements and cooldown records locally. A game account is not required. Tiny Tent does not operate a server for uploading your puzzle progress or a separate remote game-analytics service.

This information remains until it is replaced or removed from your device. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Removing the app can delete local progress; restoring a device backup may restore older data.

Advertising and privacy messages

Tiny Tent uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show banners on menu pages, optional rewarded ads for extra hints, and occasional ads between completed campaign puzzles. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable advertising privacy messages and choices. Ordinary puzzle play remains available without completing a rewarded ad.

Tiny Tent requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher ad-personalization and publisher first-party ID features, and sends Google's restricted-data-processing signal with its ad requests. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. These settings restrict advertising uses; they do not stop all data collection or replace consent where it is required. Google's restricted data processing guidance explains the treatment under applicable U.S. state privacy rules.

Google's SDKs may process the following information:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it. Tiny Tent does not request GPS or precise-location permission.
  • Device, app-scoped, or developer-scoped identifiers available under the device's permissions and the app's configuration.
  • Advertising data, such as ads displayed and interactions with ads.
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video interactions.
  • Crash information, performance information, and other technical diagnostics.

This information supports ad delivery, frequency capping, advertising reporting and measurement, analytics, security, fraud prevention, debugging, and operating and improving advertising services. Google's SDK privacy declarations also include advertising or marketing purposes; Tiny Tent includes those declared purposes in its App Store privacy disclosure. UMP uses approximate location, interaction, and performance information to provide privacy-message functionality. Some advertising and diagnostic information may be associated with a device or other identifier even when the app does not use cross-app targeted ads.

The recipients include Google and the advertising services involved in delivering and measuring ads. Data use depends on the service, region, device permissions, and privacy choices. These services may process information outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for its processing, retention, request channels, and controls. Tiny Tent does not set Google's retention periods.

Your privacy choices and requests

Open Tiny Tent's Settings and select Privacy options when that option is available to revisit your advertising privacy choices. In supported U.S. states, the form includes an option not to sell or share your data. The availability and content of the form depend on the region and Google's privacy-message service. If a form is unavailable or you need help, email mossmallowsupport@gmail.com.

Advertising consent and Apple's tracking permission are separate. Withholding advertising consent does not prevent ordinary puzzle play. Optional rewarded hints depend on ad availability. Your device's privacy settings may provide additional controls.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information, withdraw consent, opt out of certain disclosures, or appeal a response to a privacy request. Contact the email address above for requests concerning information held through support. We may need enough information to verify and respond to a request. You may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority.

Puzzle progress stays on your device; we cannot access or delete it remotely. Removing local app data does not itself delete information already held by Google or another service. Their privacy policies explain the controls and request channels for information they handle.

When you contact support

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you send. We use them to respond and investigate your request. Support mail is processed through Gmail. The app does not automatically attach your save file or diagnostic logs. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle requests, follow up on issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by email, subject to any information we must retain.
