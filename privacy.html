Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Tiny Tent is developed and operated by Caleb Davis. For privacy questions or requests, contact mossmallowsupport@gmail.com.

Game progress stays on your device

The app stores puzzle marks, undo history, active play time, hint use, completed levels, best star ratings, daily activity, settings, and ad-related hint entitlements and cooldown records locally. A game account is not required. Tiny Tent does not operate its own server for uploading your puzzle progress or a separate remote game-analytics service.

This local information remains until it is replaced or removed from your device. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Removing the app can delete its local progress; restored device backups may restore older data.

Advertising and consent

Tiny Tent uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show menu banners, optional rewarded ads for extra hints, and limited ads between completed campaign puzzles. Google’s User Messaging Platform manages applicable privacy messages and choices. Ordinary puzzle play is available without an ad being shown.

Google’s SDKs may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or app-scoped identifiers, advertising interactions, app interactions, crash information, and performance/diagnostic data. These may be used for ad delivery, advertising measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Data use depends on the SDK, device permissions, region, and your consent choices. Google and its advertising services may process data outside your country.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for Google’s handling, retention, and controls. Tiny Tent does not control Google’s retention periods.

Where required, a Privacy options button appears in Settings so you can revisit your choices. Advertising consent and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission are separate. The current app does not request Apple tracking permission. Device privacy settings may also provide controls. Withholding consent does not prevent ordinary puzzle play.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever message or attachments you choose to send. We use this to respond and investigate your request. Email is processed by the email service provider. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for support.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle requests, follow up on issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask for deletion of correspondence; we will handle the request subject to applicable requirements and any information we must retain.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and access logs, to provide and secure hosting. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. The site itself includes no advertising scripts, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or contact forms.

Your choices and requests

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact the address above for information held through support. Puzzle data stays locally on your device; we cannot access it remotely. For information controlled by Google or GitHub, their privacy policies describe their available request channels and controls.

Audience and policy updates

Tiny Tent is a general-audience puzzle game. If you have a concern about a child’s information, please contact us. We may update this policy as the app or its services change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
