Meet Tiny Tent

A cozy iPhone puzzle game with 120 campaign puzzles and daily puzzles from a rotating pool of 30. Place tents beside trees, match the row and column counts, and keep tents from touching.

Take your time. There are no lives or time penalties. Explained hints help when you’re stuck, and replays let you improve your star rating.
