Tiny Tent support

Questions, feedback, or a puzzle that doesn’t look right? Email mossmallowsupport@gmail.com.

The rules

  • Pair each tree with one tent directly above, below, left, or right of it. Each tent pairs with one tree.
  • Tents cannot touch, even diagonally.
  • Counters show tents placed / tents needed: 0/2 needs two tents; 0/0 needs none. A row or column can need several tents.
  • Grass is an erasable “no tent here” note. Filling every unused square with grass is optional.
  • Tap a selected mark again to erase it, or use Undo. Larger boards support scrolling and pinch-to-zoom; marks work at every zoom.

Hints and stars

Hints prioritize verified tent placements and explain the reasoning. One free hint is available per campaign puzzle or dated daily. Further hints can use an earned rewarded-ad hint; when unavailable, active-play cooldown rules apply. Ads are not required to finish a puzzle.

Earn three stars with no hints, two with one hint, or one with two or more hints. Your best score is kept on replay. Undo and taking your time do not reduce stars.

Daily puzzles

Daily puzzles change at midnight UTC and use a rotating 30-puzzle pool. A saved unfinished daily keeps its original date. The calendar shows daily activity. After finishing a daily, Home’s Continue moves into the campaign.

Progress and troubleshooting

Progress and settings are saved on your device. There is no game account or built-in cloud-sync service. Removing the app can remove local progress. The app attempts to restore a valid backup when it detects an unreadable save.

If an ad is unavailable, you can continue ordinary puzzle play. Internet access is needed for ads, not for the bundled campaign puzzles.

Report a problem

Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, level number, and what happened. A screenshot can help; crop out personal information. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or advertising identifiers.
